Live Highlighter is ready!

Installed

Highlight any words or phrases on every page you visit — instantly, as you browse.

Get started in 3 steps

1

Open the options page

Click the button below, or click the Live Highlighter icon in your browser toolbar and then Manage Groups.

2

Create a highlight group

Click Add Group, give it a name (e.g. "Errors"), and choose a highlight colour.

3

Add words

Expand the group and type the words or phrases you want highlighted. They will light up on every page you visit — no refresh needed.

Tips

  • Multiple groups: Create separate groups for different topics — each with its own colour.
  • Phrases: Wrap phrases in quotes, e.g. "out of memory", to highlight them as a whole.
  • Regex: Enable the Regex option on a group for advanced pattern matching.
  • Priority: Drag groups to reorder them — higher groups win when words overlap.
  • Toggle off: Use the switch in the popup or options page to pause highlighting at any time.
