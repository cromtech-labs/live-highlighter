# Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important. Live Highlighter is designed with privacy as a core principle.

## Our Commitment

**We don't collect, track, or transmit any of your data. Period.**

## What Data Does Live Highlighter Access?

### Local Storage Only

Live Highlighter stores your highlight rules in your browser's local storage. This data:

- ✅ Stays on your device
- ✅ Is never transmitted to any server
- ✅ Is never shared with third parties
- ✅ Is only accessible to the extension

### Web Page Content

To perform highlighting, Live Highlighter needs to:

- Read text content on web pages you visit
- Modify the appearance of matching text (add highlight colours)

**Important:** This content is processed entirely on your device and is never sent anywhere.

## What Data We DON'T Collect

- ❌ Browsing history
- ❌ Personal information
- ❌ Website content
- ❌ Highlight rules
- ❌ Usage statistics
- ❌ Analytics data
- ❌ Crash reports
- ❌ Any other data

## Permissions Explained

Live Highlighter requests the following browser permissions:

### Storage Permission

**Purpose:** Save your highlight rules locally in your browser

**Privacy impact:** None - data stays on your device

### Active Tab Permission

**Purpose:** Access the current page's content to apply highlights

**Privacy impact:** None - processing happens locally, no data is transmitted

### Scripting Permission

**Purpose:** Inject highlighting code into web pages

**Privacy impact:** None - the code only applies visual highlights, doesn't collect data

## Third-Party Services

**We use zero third-party services:**

- No analytics (Google Analytics, etc.)
- No error tracking (Sentry, etc.)
- No CDNs
- No external APIs
- No advertising networks

## Data Retention

Your highlight rules are stored indefinitely in your browser's local storage until you:

- Delete them manually through the extension
- Uninstall the extension
- Clear your browser data

## Uninstalling the Extension

When you uninstall Live Highlighter:

1. The extension is removed from your browser
2. All stored highlight rules are deleted
3. No trace of the extension remains
